# Privacy Policy — Simple FAQ

**Effective date:** 7 May 2026
**Last updated:** 7 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Simple FAQ (the "Extension"), a free Google Chrome browser extension published by ClickPopMe ("we", "us", "our"), handles your information.

## Summary

- The Extension is a free, personal-use utility.
- We do not collect, transmit, sell, share, or have access to any of your data.
- All Extension data is stored locally in your own browser.
- The Extension makes no network requests, contacts no external servers, and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising code.
- The Extension requests **no permissions**.

## What the Extension does

Simple FAQ is a tool that lets you view, search, and organise your own list of FAQ entries — supplied by you — in a cleaner, searchable interface than a raw spreadsheet or text file. You can import your existing entries from a CSV file and export them back to CSV at any time. Entries you create or import are visible only to you and are stored only on the device where you created or imported them.

## Information we collect

**None.** We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, we do not collect:

- Personally identifiable information (name, email address, phone number, account identifiers, etc.)
- Authentication credentials, passwords, or tokens
- Health, financial, or payment information
- Personal communications (emails, messages, chats)
- Location data (GPS, IP-based, or otherwise)
- Browsing history or web activity
- Website content or DOM data from pages you visit
- Usage analytics, performance telemetry, or crash reports
- Device information, system specifications, or fingerprinting data

## Information you create

You may enter content into the Extension — FAQ titles, descriptions, categories, and tags. This content is stored entirely on your local device using your browser's `localStorage` mechanism, scoped to the Extension. We do not have access to it. It is not transmitted to any server we operate, nor to any third party.

If you uninstall the Extension, clear your browser's site data for the Extension, or move to a different device, the data you entered will not follow you. The Extension provides an **Export CSV** feature so you can back up your own data at any time. Backups you export are saved to your device's default browser download location and are never accessible to us.

## Permissions

In its `manifest.json` declaration, the Extension requests **no permissions**. It cannot read your browsing history, open tabs, the content of websites you visit, your clipboard, your downloads, your bookmarks, your saved passwords, or any other data on your device beyond its own local storage.

## Remote code

The Extension does not load, fetch, execute, or evaluate any code from a remote source at runtime. All JavaScript, CSS, and other resources are bundled into the Extension package at the time of submission to the Chrome Web Store and are reviewed by Google as part of the approval process.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The Extension does not set cookies, web beacons, pixels, or any similar tracking technologies. The browser's built-in `localStorage` is used solely to persist FAQ entries you create; it is not transmitted off-device and is not used for tracking.

## Third-party services

The Extension does not use any third-party services, SDKs, libraries that contact external servers, fonts loaded from a CDN, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or A/B testing tools.

## Children's privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in jurisdictions where that is the applicable age). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone — children or adults — because we do not collect data at all.

## International data transfer

Because we do not collect or transmit any data, no international data transfers occur. Data you create stays on your local device and is subject to whatever protections your local jurisdiction provides for data on personal devices.

## Your rights

You have full control over data created within the Extension because that data lives only on your device:

- **Access** — open the Extension to view all your entries.
- **Export** — use Settings → Export CSV to download all your data.
- **Modify** — edit any entry directly in the Extension.
- **Delete** — delete entries individually, or clear the Extension's site data via your browser settings to remove everything.
- **Portability** — exported CSVs are standard UTF-8 and can be opened in any spreadsheet application or imported into other tools.

You do not need to contact us to exercise any of these rights, because we have no role in the storage of your data.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, the changes will be published to this repository and the "Last updated" date at the top of the document will be revised. Material changes affecting how the Extension handles data will additionally be noted in the Extension's release notes on the Chrome Web Store.

We will not retroactively change the policy to reduce the protections that applied to data you previously created.
